# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in FindIt AI respectful and welcoming.

Examples of expected behaviour:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy when sharing images, logs, or examples.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disclosure of another person's private information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or reject contributions that violate these expectations.
